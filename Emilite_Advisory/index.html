Sum Assured Basis

Uses the shared Annual Amount and Contribution Years from Growth + Flex, plus the client's age, gender and smoker status. Additional Investment Account values do not affect Sum Assured.
Death Benefit % = min(103 + 3 × (Year − 1), 160). Sum-at-Risk = max(0, Death Benefit − Accumulated Contribution). Yearly Assurance Charge = (Sum-at-Risk ÷ 1000) × 0.0834 × Assurance Rate × 12.
This illustration is for discussion purposes only and does not constitute a contract or an offer. Projected values are based on the assumed fund price and dividend payout per unit, which are not guaranteed. Past performance is not indicative of future performance.
